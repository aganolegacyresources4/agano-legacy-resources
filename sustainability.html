Sustainability & stewardship

Mineral wealth carries
a duty of stewardship.

Our responsibility extends to the land, the people who work it and the communities whose futures are connected to it.

Our approach

Build the enterprise.
Strengthen its foundations.

AGANO’s approach centres on strengthening operational governance, pursuing statutory compliance, improving environmental safeguards and building fair, sustainable livelihoods for mining teams.

For us, legacy is measured in productive operations, trusted partnerships, dignified livelihoods and opportunity preserved for those who come after us.
