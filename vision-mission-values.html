Purpose & principles

Wealth with purpose.
Principles that endure.

A long-term ambition shaped by Zimbabwean capability, resource stewardship and responsibility across generations.

Vision & mission

A clear ambition.
A lasting responsibility.

Our vision

An enduring Zimbabwean enterprise.

To build a mining and metallurgical enterprise that contributes to Zimbabwe’s industrial capability, creates wealth with purpose and preserves opportunity across generations.

Our mission

Develop resources with discipline and care.

To connect mining and processing capability with stronger governance, responsible stewardship, trusted partnerships and fair, sustainable livelihoods.

The principles behind the work

What guides our decisions.

Integrity

Build trust through clear communication, honest information and responsible follow-through.

Safety

Keep people, competence and risk awareness central to the work.

Stewardship

Recognise the responsibilities that mineral wealth brings to land, people and future generations.

Accountability

Strengthen operational discipline and take ownership of responsibilities.

Excellence

Develop the mining and metallurgical craft through learning and focused improvement.

Zimbabwean capability

Back the knowledge, skill and potential of Zimbabwean people and industry.

Partnership

Build relationships that support productive operations and lasting shared value.

Legacy

Measure progress through livelihoods, opportunity and the principles passed to those who follow.

Build with purpose

A shared conversation.
A longer horizon.
