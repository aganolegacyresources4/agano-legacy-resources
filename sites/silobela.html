Pagamesa Antimony Mine

Pagamesa- Antimony Mine

ZIMBABWE

The “Stronghold” asset connects the historic Fortuna, Pagamesa and Gothic reefs across three operating shafts.

The operation

Pagamesa.
A focused operating perspective.

This mine is named after the historic rich antimony belt of Silobela, Gothic. However, this, what we like to call it, stronghold asset houses prestigious historic reefs of Fortuna, Pagamesa and Gothic. The mine produces grades of up to 28% Stibnite. Situated on the Gothic, because it is situated on the Pagamesa and Fortuna belts, this mine houses three shafts in operation earning it its name, Strong-hold.

Company-supplied operational information, received 22 October 2026. Reported grades and development targets are not a mineral resource or reserve estimate. Request supporting technical information for a specific assessment.
