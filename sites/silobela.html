Pagamesa

Silobela, Gothic, ZIMBABWE

Nicknamed the Stronghold, Pagamesa mine connects the historic Fortuna, Pagamesa and Gothic reefs across four operating shafts.

The operation

Pagamesa mine
A focused operating perspective

Pagamesa mine works the historic Fortuna, Pagamesa and Gothic reefs through four operating shafts—three focused on antimony and one on gold. Reported ore grades are 32% stibnite and 10 grams of gold per tonne. These figures describe the mineral content of the ore; the amount recovered during processing depends on the ore’s characteristics and the efficiency of the recovery process.

From reef to recovery

Pagamesa’s antimony ore supplies AGANO’s processing plant in Sebakwe, Kwekwe, connecting underground extraction with the production of antimony concentrate.
