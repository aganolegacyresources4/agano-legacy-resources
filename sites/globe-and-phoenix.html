LonRho

Kwekwe, Globe & Phoenix

The LonRho mine is located within Kwekwe’s historic Globe & Phoenix complex. The mine has one shaft located 500 meters due-west of the historic John Bull shaft.

The operation

LonRho Mine
A focused operating perspective

Situated in Kwekwe within the Globe & Phoenix complex. The shaft targets a network of gold and antimony reefs of this historic mining site. It currently produces 10 grams a tonne in gold and 35-45% SB for antimony. LonRho is currently on level 2 at 44 meters and promises to be our best producing shaft.
