Anzac Antimony Mine

Anzac Antimony Mine

An antimony project in the Sebakwe block, with reef development directed towards the main Jojo ore body.

The operation

Anzac.
A focused operating perspective.

Anzac is located north of Kwekwe in the Sebakwe block of Zimbabwe’s Midlands Province. It forms part of the feed network associated with our Sebakwe antimony processing facility.

A minor reef has been intersected at 18 metres. The development objective is to intersect the main Jojo ore body at a target depth of 60 metres.

That target describes the intended next development outcome. Our focus is on strengthening the geological understanding, planning the work carefully and connecting mine development with the requirements of the processing route.

Company-supplied operational information, received 22 October 2026. Reported grades and development targets are not a mineral resource or reserve estimate. Request supporting technical information for a specific assessment.
