Procurement & suppliers

Relevant capability.
Responsible relationships.

A strong supplier conversation connects a real business need with clear information about capability, scope and delivery.

Supplier engagement

Introduce what your business does well.

AGANO welcomes concise, relevant supplier introductions. A useful first message describes your principal offering, the locations you can serve and the business context you are seeking to support. It should help establish whether a further conversation is appropriate without requiring confidential commercial files.

01

Equipment & engineering

Equipment, technical support, engineering inputs and related supply capabilities.

02

Operations & logistics

Industrial consumables, transport, logistics and practical operational support.

03

Professional & responsible services

Safety, environmental, information technology and professional service capabilities.

Supplier enquiries

Start with a clear introduction.

01

Define the offering

Be specific about the products or services, relevant experience and the purpose of the enquiry.

02

Keep the first message proportionate

Do not include bank details, sensitive pricing, identity documents or large attachments.

03

Understand the process

A supplier enquiry does not constitute registration, qualification, a purchase order or an award of business.

Tender submissions must follow the instructions in an actual tender notice. This enquiry channel is for business introductions and procurement questions.
