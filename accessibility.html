Accessibility

A clearer experience
for more people.

Readable content, keyboard access and understandable interactions are part of the way this website is designed.

Our design target

The website is designed with WCAG 2.2 Level AA as its accessibility target. That is a design objective, not a claim of certification or a guarantee that every criterion has been independently verified.

Navigating the site

Every main page contains its own navigation and content. A skip link leads to the main content, current sections are identified and visible focus is provided. Links and disclosure menus can be operated by keyboard.

Forms and message review

Forms use visible labels, required-field information and text feedback. The message review can be reached by keyboard. Nothing is automatically sent; you have an opportunity to read or copy the prepared text before opening WhatsApp.

Images and motion

Images have text alternatives or are treated as decorative where appropriate. The image viewer supports Escape and arrow-key navigation. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Verification status

Source structure, internal references, validation and message-routing logic are checked automatically. Browser-based assistive-technology testing, device-level reflow checks and independent accessibility assessment have not yet been completed in this environment.

If something is difficult to use

You can copy a prepared message and use WhatsApp directly if the handoff does not work on your device. Raise accessibility feedback through the general enquiry category or your existing AGANO contact, describing the page and the difficulty without including sensitive information.
