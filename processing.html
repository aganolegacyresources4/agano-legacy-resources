Antimony Processing Plant

Our Sebakwe, Kwekwe facility is designed to produce antimony concentrate from AGANO’s stated feed network.

Sebakwe processing facility

Bringing mine feed
and metallurgy together

Located in Sebakwe, Kwekwe, our facility is designed to produce antimony concentrate. We source our feed ore from Pagamesa mine, Anzac & LonRho reefs. The processing streamline includes gravity concentration- Alkaline Leach- electro-winning- Smelting.

Location
Sebakwe, Kwekwe, Zimbabwe
Designed output
Antimony concentrate and Antimony Ingot Metal
Stated feed sources
Pagamesa mine · Anzac · LonRho
