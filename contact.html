Before you continue

A clear handoff to WhatsApp

Is my message sent automatically?

No. The website prepares your text. You review it, open AGANO’s WhatsApp conversation and press Send yourself.

Does this website store my enquiry?

The website does not save your message in a database or browser storage. WhatsApp and your browser handle the message and link according to their own settings and policies.

Should I include sensitive information?

No. Keep this introduction non-confidential. Do not include passwords, identity records, banking details, sensitive coordinates or documents.

Can I edit the message?

Yes. You can return to the form before opening WhatsApp, or edit the prepared text in WhatsApp before sending.

What if WhatsApp does not open?

Use Copy message, then open WhatsApp yourself and paste the text into the appropriate conversation. No delivery is claimed by this website.
