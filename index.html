About AGANO

A Zimbabwean mining
and mineral processing
enterprise.

AGANO Legacy Resources, formerly RIFA Legacy Resources, is an independent Zimbabwean mining house focused on gold and antimony extraction, beneficiation and processing. With assets around Kwekwe and Silobela, we are developing productive operations and processing capability to create long-term value for stakeholders.

Our mineral portfolio

Antimony

Antimony is a strategic focus for AGANO, connecting resource development with opportunities across the mineral value chain. Our approach brings together stibnite extraction, ore beneficiation and the development of processing capabilities to improve mineral recovery and product quality. Through this integrated approach, we aim to strengthen local value addition and position AGANO as a reliable supplier to international markets. Our development priorities centre on consistent production, technical discipline and products aligned with customer specifications. As global industries seek dependable sources of this critical mineral, we are strengthening our local operational and beneficiation foundations to support long-term commercial partnerships.

Gold

Gold is an integral part of AGANO’s mineral portfolio, with its association with stibnite presenting both processing challenges and opportunities for value recovery. Our ores include refractory material that requires treatment suited to its mineralogical characteristics. We take pride in our processing plant’s ability to recover gold through gravity separation and pyrometallurgical treatment, bringing together complementary methods to address these complex ores. This processing capability strengthens local beneficiation and enables us to pursue greater value from both the gold and antimony within our deposits.
