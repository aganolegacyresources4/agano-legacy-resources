Mining operations

From the working reef
to the processing route

Four mines, two core mineral interests and a connected approach to extraction and metallurgy.

The operating model

An integrated mineral value chain

AGANO Legacy Resources is a premier, independent Zimbabwean mining house specializing in the extraction and processing of high-grade gold and antimony assets. Driven by an elite team of 300 skilled mining and metallurgical professionals, our enterprise operates a highly resilient physical asset network across the historic Silobela-Gothic, Pagamesa mine and Fortuna complex, Sebakwe- Anzac bloc, and Globe&Phoenix LonRho reef, and the Great Dyke Sherwood mine. We manage the entire mineral value chain internally—from deep-level extraction to advanced processing—delivering premium, market-ready products to the global industrial market.
