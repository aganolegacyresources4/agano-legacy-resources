Website information

A clear context
for using this website

Use this material as a corporate introduction and a starting point for an appropriate business conversation.

Purpose of the website

This website introduces AGANO Legacy Resources, our business perspective and the routes for stakeholder engagement. It provides general information rather than project-specific technical, financial or contractual advice.

Technical and commercial information

A reference to a commodity, mining method or development stage is not a statement that AGANO owns a particular asset, holds mineral rights or has an attributable resource or reserve. Specific claims should be read with the relevant current source documentation.

Business discussions

A website enquiry does not create a contract, confirm a commercial engagement, register a supplier, submit a tender or establish an employment relationship. Those matters require the appropriate separate process and terms.

WhatsApp messages

The website prepares text for your review. You choose to open AGANO’s WhatsApp conversation, review the recipient and send. It does not automatically send, monitor or verify delivery of your message.

Images and editorial material

Contextual mining, geology and landscape visuals are clearly identified as illustrative imagery. They do not depict verified AGANO properties or personnel. Articles are general editorial perspectives, not announcements of specific projects or performance results.

External services

Following a WhatsApp link or another external destination takes you to a separate service. Check the destination and the recipient, and consider the information you choose to share.

Keeping the conversation useful

Contact AGANO with a clear, relevant and non-confidential introduction. Use an appropriate formal channel before exchanging sensitive files, making commitments or relying on project-specific information.
