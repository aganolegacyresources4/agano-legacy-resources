Introduce your professional interests

Use this form for a general career conversation or a question about advertised opportunities. Explain your area of interest, relevant background and the purpose of your enquiry.

This is not an application to a specific open vacancy. A role-specific application should follow the instructions in the relevant advertisement. Do not send CVs, certificates, identity documents or banking information through this initial form.

Does a career enquiry confirm a vacancy?

No. It starts a professional conversation. The availability, location and terms of any role must be established through the relevant recruitment process.

Can I attach documents?

This website does not collect uploads. Share documents only through an appropriate recruitment channel when requested.
